### Projects

**[nba-auction-draft](https://github.com/Gaarbra/nba-auction-draft)**: a fantasy-style NBA auction draft app, paired with **[hoop-bids-data-refresher](https://github.com/Gaarbra/hoop-bids-data-refresher)**, a PowerShell pipeline that keeps live stats in sync.

**REC-Research-Project-2026**: panel analysis of zero-emission vehicle (ZEV) adoption and air quality across 20 Southern California cities, 2021 to 2024 (80 city-year observations). Used OLS with year fixed effects and city-clustered standard errors, applying the Frisch-Waugh-Lovell theorem to address multicollinearity across four model specifications. Finding: at current adoption rates (1 to 7% of registered vehicles), ZEV penetration shows no measurable association with air quality improvement. Pre-existing environmental burden (CalEnviroScreen score) is the stronger predictor, pointing toward heavy-duty diesel freight, not passenger vehicles, as the main driver of pollution disparities in disadvantaged communities.
